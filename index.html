Bilgi Dozu Uploader

Bilgi Dozu Uploader is a private publishing tool used by the creator of the Bilgi Dozu (Turkish) and Fact Dose (English) short educational video channels.

The creator produces short, vertical "did you know?" videos about science, history, space and nature. After a video is finished, the tool uploads it to the creator's own TikTok account using the official TikTok Content Posting API, so the same video does not have to be uploaded by hand on every platform.

How it works

  1. The creator connects their own TikTok account once with "Continue with TikTok" (TikTok Login Kit).
  2. When a new video is ready, the tool sends it to the creator's TikTok account, together with a caption and hashtags.
  3. The creator can review every post in the TikTok app. Videos are marked as AI-generated content.

The tool only works with accounts owned by the creator. It does not access other users' data.
