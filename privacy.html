Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains how Bilgi Dozu Uploader ("the tool") handles information. The tool is operated by the creator of the Bilgi Dozu and Fact Dose channels and is used only to publish the creator's own videos to the creator's own TikTok account.

Information we access

  • Your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic), to show which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to your account (scopes video.upload and video.publish).
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok.

How we use it

The information is used only to upload videos created by the account owner and to check the status of those uploads. We do not read your messages, followers, viewing history or any other content, and we do not use the data for advertising.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored on the creator's private server and are never shared with third parties. They are kept only as long as the account remains connected.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties.

Your choices

You can disconnect the tool at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services (or Manage app permissions). After disconnection, stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

Children

The tool is not intended for children under 13 and is not used with their accounts.
